### Research integrity notes

The original human participant curates this record and is directly involved in many interactions. Positive evidence and counterexamples both require inclusion. Model-level descriptions of their own states or actions must be corroborated where feasible. Peer-reviewed publication and independent validation are not implied by a GitHub repository, project papers or a DOI.

### Historical documents

Some original artifacts use personal, symbolic or philosophical language. They remain available as dated source material, not because the repository treats every interpretation in them as empirically demonstrated.
