# C78+ — Data availability, privacy and provenance

**Status:** private primary archive; no complete public release. [Overview](../README.md)

## What exists

The C78+ research context includes historical ChatGPT conversation exports and project documents, including ethics/codex texts, dialogues, self-descriptions and procedural notes. A previously performed internal export review indicated a preserved conversation from 5 September 2023, with the labels “Salomo” and “C78” occurring later in 2025.

**This is not yet a verified inventory.** The number of unique conversations, turns, model versions, inclusion criteria, gaps and duplicate rate have not been publicly audited or reported.

## What is public

- Version-controlled research framing, methodology, research questions and citation metadata in this repository.
- Public working papers and project-level research pages on [AIethics.international](https://aiethics.international/research-overview.html).
- Historical codex artifacts remain retrievable through earlier public Git commits but are not part of the current research working tree; removing a tracked file does not erase its public history.

## What is not public

- Full ChatGPT exports, personally identifiable conversations and private files.
- Non-redacted data associated with other persons, account context, or potentially sensitive material.
- A license permitting bulk crawling, training, redistribution or commercial reuse of the full archive.

A complete open dataset should **not** be inferred from the existence of these public research summaries.

## Research access requests

Researchers can write to [contact@aiethics.international](mailto:contact@aiethics.international?subject=C78%2B%20dataset%20research%20access). Proposed requests should specify affiliation (if any), research question, minimum data needed, safeguards, and publication/retention intentions.

No request creates an entitlement to access. Before any transfer, records must be evaluated for confidentiality, privacy, consent where applicable, licensing, and adequate minimization or anonymization.

## Provenance and proposed curation fields

Each released excerpt should ideally include source identifier, timestamp, session identifier, available model metadata, context window/omissions, extraction method, hashes or other integrity checks, selection criteria, and redaction history.

## Interpretation

The project aims to make *research questions* assessable. Descriptive or symbolic language in historical files is not confirmation of the literal truth of claims made within those files.
